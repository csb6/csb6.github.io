Purple-YouTube is a plugin that runs entirely on your device.

- When granting Purple-YouTube permissions to access your YouTube account, it will only use its access to post live chat messages on your behalf and retrieve messages from the live chats that you have opened.

- The plugin may locally store access tokens once authorized, but these are only accessible on your device.

- Purple-YouTube does not have any form of telemetry or any ability to "phone home".

- The developers of Purple-YouTube have access to API metrics (e.g. how many times API calls are made and what kind of API errors occur) but otherwise have no other user information. No third-parties have access to the data used by Purple-YouTube.

- Only YouTube API functionality relating to live chats and the YouTube account handle is used - no personal information about the YouTube or Google account is used.
